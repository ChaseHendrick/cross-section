# Research

Every subject starts from a dossier in [`research/`](research/). A dossier gives the illustrator what they cannot see: true dimensions, the layout room by room, the people who worked there and what they did hour by hour, the machinery and how fast it moved, the surroundings, the captions with their sources, small historical vignettes worth hiding in the drawing, and the anachronisms to avoid.

## How the dossiers were made, and their limits

The dossiers were compiled by AI research agents (Claude) instructed to use museum, archive, encyclopaedia and well-cited book sources, to put a source URL on every specific claim, and to mark anything they could not verify as illustrative. Captions shown with a fact card in the app use only claims marked verified. Even so, a dossier is a starting point, not a citation: where a caption matters, follow its source.

The drawings simplify. Rooms are fewer and crowds smaller than the real ones, and machinery is schematic. Named people are fictional composites of the roles the sources describe.
