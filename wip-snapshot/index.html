    How to explore

    On a touch screen
    Look aroundDrag with one finger
    ZoomPinch, or double-tap to zoom in (in the slice tool a tap places a cut). Whole (under More on a phone) shows the whole subject
    Turn the viewTwist two fingers, or tap the view button (Book view; under More on a phone) for the next viewing angle
    Follow someoneTap a person, or choose from People. Tap × on their card to let them go
    FactsTap a framed caption, or choose from People, then Facts
    With a keyboard and mouse
    Look aroundDrag, or use the arrow keys
    Turn the viewRight-drag or Shift-drag, or V for the next viewing angle
    ZoomScroll, pinch, double-click, or + −. 0 shows the whole subject
    Follow someoneClick or tap a person, or choose from W (who is here). Esc lets them go
    CaptionsClick a framed caption for the full story, or find it in W under Facts. L hides or shows captions
    SliceS opens the slice tool: click to place cuts, drag to move them, × to remove. O opens or closes the slices. Zero cuts is allowed
    TimeDrag the clock. N night, D day, K clock speed, Space pause
    Guided tourT starts it, ←/→ step through while it runs
    AmbientA drifts unattended through every subject; any key or click returns
    ContentsC chooses another subject
    WeatherR steps through clear, rain, storm, fog and snow
    SoundM turns the soundscape on or off. Zoom in on a machine to hear it
    PictureP saves the current view as a PNG at twice screen resolution
    Full screenF enters or leaves full screen
    Step backEsc closes the top panel, leaves the slice tool, ends the tour, or lets the person you follow go
